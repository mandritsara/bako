Adverbial Phrase of Place (continued 2)

Malagasy–Tsimihety with Bako

F. Common Prepositions of Place

Preposition (off) / (tsm) Preposition (off) / (tsm)
Ambony ON — ABOVE Anelanelana BETWEEN
Ambany UNDER — BENEATH Akaiky (off) / Marikitry (tsm) NEXT TO — NEAR — BESIDE
Aloha (off) / Alôhaka (tsm) BEFORE Lavitra (off) / Lavitry (tsm) FAR
Aoriana (off) / Afara (tsm) AFTER — BEHIND Ampita ACROSS
Anoloana (off) / Alôhaka (tsm) IN FRONT OF Amorony — Sisiny EDGE — BORDER
Andamosina — Ambadika (off) / Afara (tsm) BEHIND Manodidina AROUND
Afovoany (off) / Anivo (tsm) IN THE MIDDLE Ivelany (off) / Ambelany (tsm) OUTSIDE
Anaty INSIDE — IN Ambody AT THE FOOT OF
Antampony AT THE TOP OF Antendrony AT THE TIP OF

Examples

Manodidina ny boaty ny paoma.

(The apples are around the box)

Anatin'ny boaty ny akoholahy.

(The rooster is in the box)

Andamosin'ny boaty ny akohovavy. (off)

Afaran'ny boaty ny akohovavy. (tsm)

(The hen is behind the box)

Ambonin'ny boaty ny zanak'akoho.

(The chicken is on the box)

There are 4 possible questions:

  • — AIZA ny ……? (where is …… / where are …… ?)
  • — INONA no ……? (off) / INO ……? (tsm) (what is / what are …… ?)
  • — IZA no ……? (off) / ZÔVY ……? (tsm) (who is / who are …… ?)
  • — …… VE ……? (is …… / are …… / do …… ?)

Manodidina ny boaty ny paoma.

The apples are around the box.

Afovoan'ny paoma ny boaty.

The box is in the middle of the apples.

AIZA ny paoma? — Manodidina ny boaty.

AIZA ny boaty? — Afovoan'ny paoma.

INONA no manodidina ny boaty? — paoma

INONA no afovoan'ny paoma? — boaty

Manodidina ny boaty VE ny paoma? — Eny, manodidina.

Afaran'ny kakazo ny ankizy.

The children are behind the tree.

Alôhaka ny ankizy ny kakazo.

The tree is in front of the children.

AIZA ny ankizy? — Afaran'ny kakazo.

AIZA ny kakazo? — Alôhaka ny ankizy.

ZÔVY afaran'ny kakazo? — ny ankizy

INO alôhaka ny ankizy? — ny kakazo

Afaran'ny ankizy VE ny kakazo? — Ehe, alôhaka.

Answers — Exercise 1

Items 1–4 (rooster / table):

1. Ambany latabatra (under the table)

2. Aorian'ny latabatra (behind the table)

3. Akaikin'ny latabatra (next to the table)

4. Ambony latabatra (on top of the table)

Items 5–7 (rooster / hen / chicken / box):

5a. Ambony boaty    5b. Anaty boaty    5c. Alôhaka boaty

6a. Anaty boaty    6b. Afaran'ny boaty    6c. Ambony boaty

7a. Ambony boaty    7b. Anoloan'ny boaty    7c. Aorian'ny boaty

8. Anaty vera ny sotrokely (the teaspoon is inside the glass)

9. Ivelan'ny vera ny sotrokely / Akaikin'ny sotrokely ny vera

Answers — Exercise 2

1. Afovoan'ny seza ny latabatra. — The table is in the middle of the chairs.

2. Anelanelan'ny hazo 2 ny seza. — The chair is between two trees.

3. Mamaky boky ambodin'ny hazo ny ankizy. — The children are reading books at the foot of a tree.

4. Mamaky boky ambony hazo izy. — She is reading a book up in a tree.

5. Anaty harona ny paoma. — The apple is in the basket.

6. Manodidina ny hazo ny ankizy. — The children are around the tree.

7. Anoloan'ny fitaratra izy. — She is in front of the mirror.

8. Ivelan'ny varavarana izy. — He is outside the door.

9. Amin'ny sisin'ny latabatra ny sarintany. — The globe is on the edge of the table.

10. Akaikin'ny vera ny sotrokely. — The teaspoon is next to the glass.
