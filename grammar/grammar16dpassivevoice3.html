1- Comparing active and passive sentences

Every Malagasy verb has active and passive forms (except the so-called intransitive verbs, which are verbs of state).

In the active voice, whoever does the action is at the end of the sentence and the object comes immediately after the verb. (subject)

In the passive voice, whoever does the action (subject) appears as a suffix (agent) on the verb, and the object, which is highlighted, becomes the subject at the end.

  • Active voice (V.A)
    Active verb  –  object  –  subject
  • Passive voice (V.P)
    Passive verb  –  subject / agent (suffix)  –  object
