Malagasy–Tsimihety Grammar Lesson 16B

The Passive Voice (continued)

Malagasy–Tsimihety with Bako

1. The passive form of verbs

Every Malagasy verb has a root. The passive form of the verb comes from this root:

  • By adding the prefix A- to the root  (A + verb root)

OR

  • By adding the suffix -INA / -ANA to the root  (verb root + INA/ANA)

OR

  • Sometimes the root itself, which is called the 'root passive'  (verb root)

NB: It is good to know these 3 ways, but the most effective method is to learn each verb by using it and listening to it.
