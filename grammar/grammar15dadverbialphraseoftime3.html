Malagasy–Tsimihety Grammar Lesson 15D

Adverbial Phrase of Time (continued 2)

Malagasy–Tsimihety with Bako

Time Expressions Answering "At What Time?"

Present / Future: Amin'ny firy no …? (off) / Amin'ny firy …? (tsm)

Past: Tamin'ny firy no …? (off) / Tamin'ny firy …? (tsm)

Examples:

Amin'ny firy izy mionjogno? — Amin'ny 6.

(At what time does he/she wake up? — At 6 o'clock.)

Tamin'ny firy izy nandry? — Tamin'ny 10 sy sasany.

(At what time did he/she go to bed? — At half past 10.)

Vocabulary — Meals

Homagna maraindraigny — to have breakfast (lit. to eat in the morning)

Homagna matsagna — to have lunch (lit. to eat at midday)

Homagna amin'ny aligny — to have dinner (lit. to eat at night)

Exercise — translate into your own language:

Homagna maraindraigny zahay amin'ny 6.

→ We have breakfast at 6 o'clock.

Homagna matsagna zahay amin'ny 12.

→ We have lunch at 12 o'clock.

Homagna amin'ny aligny izahay amin'ny 7.

→ We have dinner at 7 pm.

Answers to Lesson 15 Exercises

Exercise A — "Miasa izy" (He / she works)

Niasa izy omaly.

→ He / she worked yesterday.

Tsy miasa izy androany.

→ He / she is not working today.

Hiasa izy afaka rahampitso.

→ He / she will work the day after tomorrow.

Hiasa ve izy amaraigny?

→ Will he / she work tomorrow?

Miasa ve izy niagny?

→ Is he / she working today?

Tafiriagna izy niasa?

→ When did he / she work?

Hafiriagna izy hiasa?

→ When will he / she work?

Oviana no tsy niasa izy? off

→ When did he / she not work?

Rahoviana no tsy hiasa izy? off

→ When will he / she not work?

Exercise B — "Mafana ny andro" (It is hot)

Nafana ny andro tamin'ny maraina.

→ It was hot in the morning.

Tsy mafana ny andro amin'ny alina.

→ It is not hot at night.

Hafana ny andro amin'ny tolakandro.

→ It will be hot in the afternoon.

Tafiriagna nafana ny andro?

→ When was it hot?

Rahoviana no hafana ny andro? off

→ When will it be hot?

Exercise C — "Tonga ny vahiny" (The visitor(s) arrived)

Tonga ny vahiny tamin'ny Talata.

→ The visitor(s) arrived on Tuesday.

Tonga ny vahiny tamin'ny Lahady.

→ The visitor(s) arrived on Sunday.

Tonga ny vahiny tamin'ny Sabotsy maraina.

→ The visitor(s) arrived on Saturday morning.

Tsy ho tonga ny vahiny amin'ny Zomā.

→ The visitor(s) will not arrive on Friday.

Ho tonga ny vahiny amin'ny Robiā.

→ The visitor(s) will arrive on Wednesday.

Tafiriagna tonga ny vahiny?

→ When did the visitor(s) arrive?

Hafiriagna ho tonga ny vahiny?

→ When will the visitor(s) arrive?

Repeat the same sentences, substituting different months:

Lafo ny vary tamin'ny volana Zanvie. (Rice was expensive in January.)

Lafo ny vary tamin'ny volana Fevrie …

Betsaka ny vahiny amin'ny volana Mey. (There are many visitors in May.)

Betsaka ny vahiny amin'ny volana Jona …

Tsy hiasa zaho amin'ny volana Desambra. (I will not work in December.)

Tsy hiasa zaho amin'ny volana Oktobra …

Annexe — Tense Agreement

Adjective agreement with tense

Past form Present (1st letter of adjective) Future form
N Nafana ny andro (It was hot) M Mafana ny andro (It is hot) H Hafana ny andro (It will be hot)
N tsm / — off Nora ny manga (tsm) Mora ny manga (off) (The mangoes were cheap) M (2-syllable word) Mora ny manga (The mangoes are cheap) Ho Ho mora ny manga (The mangoes will be cheap)
— Faly ny zaza (The child was happy) Other consonant (not M-) Faly ny zaza (The child is happy) Ho Ho faly ny zaza (The child will be happy)

Verb agreement with tense

Past form Present (1st letter of verb) Future form
N- Niasa izy (He/she worked) M- Miasa izy (He/she works) H- Hiasa izy (He/she will work)
No- Nosasana ny trano (The house was washed) Other consonant (not M-) Sasana ny trano (The house is being washed) Ho- Hosasana ny trano (The house will be washed)
— Tonga ny vahiny (The visitor had arrived) Other consonant (not M-) (root verb) Tonga ny vahiny (The visitor has arrived) Ho- Ho tonga ny vahiny (The visitor will arrive)
N- Natao ny mofo (The bread was made) Vowel Atao ny mofo (The bread is made) H- Hatao ny mofo (The bread will be made)
