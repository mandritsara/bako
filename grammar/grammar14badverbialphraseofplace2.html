Malagasy–Tsimihety Grammar Lesson 14B

Adverbial Phrase of Place (continued)

Malagasy–Tsimihety with Bako

Structure

NB: CCL = Adverbial Phrase of Place (Complément Circonstanciel de Lieu)

The structure of the CCL is:

Locative  —  Place

Position of the CCL in a Sentence

NB: TSY = negation ("not")

NB: mô = ve = question particle ("is it…? / are…?")

Pattern 1: (TSY) — CCL — Subject

(TSY) CCL Subject English
TSY aketo zareo They are not here
Tany Frantsa izahay We were in France
Aketo amin'ny lôpitaly mô are(o)? Are you here at the hospital?
TSY any an-trano izy He is not at home

Pattern 2: (TSY) Adjective — Subject — CCL

(TSY) Adjective Subject CCL English
Manintsy ny andro any Frantsa. It is cold in France.
TSY mamy ny voasary aketo. The orange is not sweet here.
Betsaka ny manga any an-trano. There are many mangoes at home.
Tsara fanahy ny mpiasa ao amin'ny hôpitaly. The hospital staff are kind.

Pattern 3: (TSY) Misy — Noun — CCL

(TSY) Misy Noun CCL English
TSY misy zavokā any Frantsa There are no avocados in France.
Misy malagasy ve aketo? Are there any Malagasy people here?

Pattern 4: (TSY) Verb … CCL — Subject

(TSY) Verb … CCL Subject English
TSY avy aketo zaho. I don't come from here.
Mipetraka any Frantsa izy. She lives in France.
TSY miasa eto amin'ny hôpitaly I Soa. Soa doesn't work here at the hospital.
Niandry anao tany an-trano zaho. I waited for you at home.

Questions

In a question sentence, the question word AIZA or TAIZA (where) can be placed either in the same position as the CCL, or at the beginning of the sentence.

  1. Avy aiza? — Where are you from? / Where does … come from?
  2. Taiza? / Nandeha taiza? — Where have you been? / Where did … go?
  3. Aiza? — Where is …?
  4. Handeha aiza? / Hankaiza? / Ho aiza? — Where are you going?

Examples:

Avy aiza ianao? — Where are you from?

Taiza / Nandeha taiza ianao? — Where have you been?

Aiza ianao? — Where are you?

Handeha aiza (hankaiza / ho aiza) ianao? — Where are you going?

Malagasy English
Eto Frantsa izy. / AIZA izy? She is in France. / WHERE is she?
Mangatsiaka (off) / Manintsy (tsm) eto Frantsa.
AIZA no mangatsiaka? (off) / AIZA manintsy? (tsm) 		It is cold in France. WHERE is it cold?
Tsy misy manga any Frantsa.
AIZA no tsy misy manga? (off) / AIZA tsy misy manga? (tsm) 		There are no mangoes in France. WHERE are there no mangoes?
Niasa tany Frantsa izy.
Niasa TAIZA izy? / TAIZA no niasa izy? (off) / TAIZA niasa izy? (tsm) 		She worked in France. Where DID she work?

Exercises

Exercise 1 — Put the words in brackets into the correct order

Translate the following sentences into Malagasy by putting the words in brackets in the correct order. Each sentence begins with the word in capitals.

He doesn't work here at the hospital. (eto – izy – TSY – miasa – hôpitaly – amin'ny)

→ TSY miasa eto amin'ny hôpitaly izy.

I am here at the bank. (zaho – banky – AKETO – amin'ny)

→ Aketo amin'ny banky zaho.

There is a celebration at the Catholic school. (any – sekoly – MISY – katolika – fety – amin'ny)

→ Misy fety any amin'ny sekoly Katolika.

Where is the bank here in Mandritsara? (ny banky – eto – AIZA – Mandritsara)

→ Aiza ny banky eto Mandritsara?

It is cold in Canada. (akany – MANINTSY – Kanada – ny andro)

→ Manintsy ny andro akany Kanada.

People don't grow rice in France. (TSY – any – vary – mamboly – Frantsa)

→ TSY mamboly vary any Frantsa.

He is at church. (am-piangonana – izy – ANY)

→ Any am-piangonana izy.

There are flowers in the garden. (MISY – akao – flera – an-jaridaina)

→ Misy flera akao an-jaridaina.

The children are playing in the courtyard. (MISOMA – an-tokontany – ny ankizy – akao)

→ Misoma akao an-tokontany ny ankizy.
