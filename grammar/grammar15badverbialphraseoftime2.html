Exercise A — Translate into English

Use the time expressions above.

Tsy niasa i Soa omaly hariva.

→ Soa didn't work yesterday evening.

Ho tonga ny vahiny amin'ny Talata alina.

→ The visitor(s) will arrive on Tuesday night.

Miasa ve ianao amin'ny hariva?

→ Do you work in the evening?

Mafana ny andro amin'ny volana Oktobra.

→ It is hot in October.

Sasana ny trano niagny maraindraigny. tsm

→ The floor was mopped this morning.

Hafana ny andro amaraigny folakandro. tsm

→ It will be hot tomorrow afternoon.

Nôra ny manga nomoaligny harivariva. tsm

→ The mangoes were cheap yesterday evening.

Tonga izy amin'ny volana Mey.

→ He / she arrived in May.

Manintsy ny andro amin'ny aligny. tsm

→ It is cold at night.

Time Expressions Answering "How Often?"

Question: Isaky ny inona no …? (off) / Isaka ino …? (tsm) — "How often …?"

Examples:

Isaky ny inona no tsy miasa izy? — Isaky ny Alarobia hariva.

(How often does he have time off? — Every Wednesday afternoon.)

Isaka ino homagna vary izy? — Isan'andro.

(How often does he eat rice? — Every day.) tsm

Isaky ny … (off) / Isaka … (tsm) English
Isaky ny maraina (off) Isaka maraindraigny (tsm) Every morning
Isaky ny Alarobia (off) Isaka Robiā (tsm) Every Wednesday
Isaky ny volana Janoary (off) Isaka vôlagna Zanvie (tsm) Every January
Isan'andro (off/tsm) Every day
Isan-kerinandro (off) Isaka herinandro (tsm) Every week
Isam-bolana (off) Isam-bôlagna (tsm) Every month
Isan-taona (off) Isan-taogno (tsm) Every year

Exercise B — Translate into English

Use the frequency expressions above and the vocabulary below.

Mihinana vary isan'andro ny malagasy.

→ The Malagasy eat rice every day.

Tsy mianatra ny mpianatra isaka Robiā harivariva. tsm

→ The students don't study every Wednesday afternoon.

Migiaka kafe izy isaka maraindraigny. tsm

→ He / she drinks coffee every morning.

Mandeha vakansy izy isaka vôlagna Jolay. tsm

→ He / she goes on holiday every July.

Mihazakazaka isan-kerinandro izy.

→ He / she runs every week.
