Transport (continued 2)

3. Useful verbs (continued)

3.1 Table 1

Malagasy English Examples
MandaloTo passMandalo eto ve ny bus 163?(Does bus 163 pass by here?)
Mandroso sy miverinaReturn (go and come back)Tapakila mandroso sy miverina 3 azafady.(3 return tickets, please.)
Mandoa (vola / frais) (off) / Magnefa vola (tsm)To payAiza no mandoa vola / Aiza no mandoa frais azafady?(Where do we pay, please?)
MividyTo buyAiza no mividy billet (tapakila) azafady?(Where do we buy the ticket, please?)

3.2 Say the following sentences in Malagasy

  • – Does the bus pass by here, please?
  • – Can we pass by the station, please?
  • – I will pay for the return ticket.
  • – This is where we pay, please.
  • – How many people do you want to buy tickets for, please?
  • – Have you already bought the ticket?
  • – Where is the ticket office, please?
  • – I would like to buy a ticket for Antsirabe, please.
  • – The ticket office is already closed.
  • – May I ask a question, please?
  • – Do you have any questions?

Answers

  • Mandalo eto ve ny bus azafady?
  • Afaka mandalo ny gara ve isika azafady?
  • Handoa ny tapakila / frais mandroso sy miverina aho.
  • Eto no mandoa vola azafady.
  • Te hividy tapakila ho an'olona firy ianao azafady?
  • Efa nividy ny tapakila ve ianareo azafady?
  • Aiza ny mpivarotra tapakila azafady?
  • Te hividy billet mandeha any Antsirabe aho azafady.
  • Efa mihidy ny mpivarotra tapakila.
  • Afaka manontany ve aho azafady?
  • Misy fanontaniana ve?
