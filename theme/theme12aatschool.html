Theme 12A — Any an-tsekoly

At School

Background

Under the reign of King Radama I (1810–1828), the French Robin and Rev. Jones of the London Missionary Society (LMS) founded the first schools in Madagascar in 1820, at the initiative of a king who wanted closer ties with Europe through the religious missionaries.

Although every person has the right to education, teaching, and training, access to education remains a challenge for different reasons — parental poverty, a bad harvest, early marriage, lack of nearby infrastructure, or competing priorities. There are still those who are illiterate or leave school early. The level of schooling remains unequal across the country. State school starts at age 6, private school at age 3. University begins at around 18.

There are three official exams: at age 10, 14, and 18.

Text — A School Day

Miditra amin'ny 7 sy sasany ny mpianatra amin'ny maraina dia mirava amin'ny 11 sy sasany. Raha vao maneno ny lakolosy amin'ny 7 sy sasany dia milahatra mahitsy tsiroaroa sy mangina ny mpianatra. Miditra moramora dia miarahaba ny mpampianatra vao mipetraka dia manomboka ny fianarana. Misy fakan-drivotra 15 minitra amin'ny 9 sy sasany. Miditra amin'ny 2 sy sasany dia mirava amin'ny 4 amin'ny folakandro.

Translation:

The students start school at 7:30 in the morning and finish at 11:30. As soon as the bell rings at 7:30, the students line up straight, two by two, and silently. They enter slowly, greet the teacher, then sit down and the lesson begins. There is a 15-minute break at 9:30. They go back in at 2:30 and finish at 4:00 in the afternoon.

Activities

  • Listen and try to understand each sentence and the text above using the vocabulary table.
  • Try to retell the same story using your own school system (entry times, break times …)
