Theme 15E — Ny fitaterana

Transport (continued and final)

4. Fifampiresahana sy voambolana (Dialogue and vocabulary)

To understand them, listen to the following dialogues and then repeat them. You can take useful expressions from them.

4.1 Mandeha taxibe (Taking a bus)

– Azafady tompoko, mandalo eto ve ny bus 163?(Excuse me, does bus 163 pass by here?)

– Aan, fa handeha aiza ianao?(No, but where are you going?)

– Ho any Ankadikely.(I am going to Ankadikely.)

– Maka bus 154 eto ianao dia miala any Andravoahangy.(Take bus 154 here, then get off at Andravoahangy.)

– Any Andravoahangy, maka bus H ianao dia tonga any Ankadikely.(From Andravoahangy, take bus H and you will arrive at Ankadikely.)

– Mila miova bus izany?(So do I need to change bus?)

– Een. Fa tsy sarotra.(Yes, but it is not difficult.)

– Misaotra tompoko.(Thank you very much.)

– Tsisy fisaorana.(You are welcome.)

4.2 Anaty lamasinina (On the train)

– Afaka manontany ve azafady?(May I ask something, please?)

– Eny tompoko.(Yes, go ahead.)

– Inona ny gara manaraka azafady?(What is the next station, please?)

– Moramanga. Aiza no miala ianao?(Moramanga. Where are you getting off?)

– Moramanga. Mijanona ao Moramanga ve ny lamasinina?(Moramanga. Does the train stop at Moramanga?)

– Een. Manala olona sy entana izy.(Yes. It lets off passengers and goods.)

– Misaotra betsaka.(Thank you very much.)

– Tsisy fisaorana.(You are welcome.)

. . . . . . .

– Tonga ianao. Eto Moramanga.(You have arrived. This is Moramanga.)

– Misaotra betsaka. Soava dia.(Thank you very much. Have a good journey.)

– Misaotra tompoko.(Thank you very much.)

4.3 Maka taxi (Taking a taxi)

– Salama tompoko. Handeha aiza ianao?(Hello! Where would you like to go?)

– Salama tompoko. Ohatrinona mandeha any Analakely?(Hello! How much is it to go to Analakely?)

– Azafady, Analakely aiza ho aiza?(Excuse me, where exactly in Analakely?)

– Eo amin'ny paositra.(Near the post office.)

– Ndao! 10 000 ar.(All right. That is 10,000 Ar.)

. . . . . . . . . . .

– Eto ve?(Is it here?)

– Een, eto. Ity ny volanao. Misaotra betsaka.(Yes, here. Here is the money. Thank you very much.)

– Misaotra tompoko.(Thank you very much.)

4.4 Mamandrika toerana (Reserving seats)

– Manahoana tompoko. Mbola misy plasy ve mandeha any Antsirabe?(Hello! Are there still seats for Antsirabe?)

– Manahoana tompoko. Handeha rahoviana?(Hello! When are you leaving?)

– Sabotsy maraina.(Saturday morning.)

– Olona firy azafady?(For how many people, please?)

– Olona 2.(For 2 people.)

. . . . . . . .

– Een, mbola malalaka aorianan'ny sofera.(Yes, there is still space behind the driver.)

– Amin'ny sisiny ve azafady?(Is it by the window, please?)

– Azafady, efa misy olona amin'ny sisiny.(Sorry, the window seat is already taken.)

– Tsy maninona. Mety. Ohatrinona ny frais?(That is fine. All right. How much does the journey cost?)

– 20 000 ar ny olona 1.(It is 20,000 Ar per person.)

– Een, ataovy.(Yes, I will take them.)

. . . . . . . . . . .

– Ity ny tapakilanareo. Amin'ny 8 no miala ny taxi-brousse. Fa mila tonga eto ny mpandeha amin'ny 7.(Here are your tickets. The taxi-brousse leaves at 8, but the passengers need to be here at 7.)

– Ity ny vola. Misaotra betsaka.(Here is the money. Thank you very much.)

– Misaotra betsaka. Amin'ny Sabotsy izany. Aza tara!(Thank you very much. So that is for Saturday. Be there on time!)
