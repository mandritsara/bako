Theme 11D — Asking for Directions (continued 3)

Manontany sy manoro làlana sy toerana — Tohiny 3

Asking for and giving directions to a road or place (continued 3)

1. Revision

See previous lessons 11A and 11B. Translate into your language.

Aiza ny banky? — Ampitan'ny fijanonan'ny bus. (Where is the bank? — Across from the bus stop.)

Aiza ny hopitaly? — Aorian'ny tetezana. (Where is the hospital? — After the bridge.)

Aiza ny tetezana? — Anelanelan'ny hôpitaly sy ny banky. (Where is the bridge? — Between the hospital and the bank.)

Aiza ny fijanonan'ny bus? — Amin'ny faran'ny fidinana. (Where is the bus stop? — At the end of the descent.)

Aiza ny fiampitana? — Anoloan'ny farmasia. (Where is the crossing? — In front of the pharmacy.)

Aiza ny farmasia? — Amin'ny zoron-dàlana ankavia. (Where is the pharmacy? — At the corner of the street on the left.)

3. Handeha any amin'ny hôpitaly i Soa

Soa is going to the hospital

A

Soa: Azafady, aiza ny làlana mankany amin'ny hôpitaly?

Fara: Mandeha mahitsy ianao. Misy fiangonana amin'ny ankavia. Mbola manohy ianao dia misy fidinana.

Soa: Dia tonga amin'ny hôpitaly ve?

Fara: Aan, mbola tsy tonga.

B

Fara: Amin'ny faran'ny fidinana, mivily ankavia ianao. Misy sampanan-dàlana dia mivily mianavaratra. Mandeha kely dia misy tetezana.

Soa: Mbola lavitra avy eo ve?

Fara: Tsy lavitra avy eo.

D

Fara: Miampita ny tetezana ianao dia manohy. Misy fiviliana ankavia fa tsy mivily. Manohy mahitsy.

Soa: Manaraka ny ankavanana ve?

Fara: Een, miakatra kely dia manohy mahitsy. Misy fiviliana amin'ny ankavia; trano misy etazy amin'ny ankavanana.

E

Soa: Ny hôpitaly ve?

Fara: Efa tsy lavitra ny hôpitaly. Avy eo amin'ny trano misy etazy ianao dia manohy mahitsy, mandalo bazary sy sekoly. Manohy kely ianao dia mahita fiviliana amin'ny ankavanana. Misy vavahady lehibe — ny vavahadin'ny hôpitaly.

F

Soa: Misaotra betsaka tompoko.

Fara: Tsisy fisaorana. Raha very ianao dia afaka manontany olona hoe "aiza ny hôpitaly azafady?"

Soa: Misaotra betsaka tompoko.

Fara: Tsisy fisaorana.

4. Translate into your own language

Work through each section. Use the vocabulary boxes to help you.

A

1. Aiza ny làlana mankany amin'ny hôpitaly? → Where is the road to the hospital?
2. Mandeha mahitsy ianao. → Go straight on.
3. Misy fiangonana amin'ny ankavia. → There is a church on the left.
4. Mbola manohy ianao dia misy fidinana. → Continue, then there is a descent.
5. Dia tonga amin'ny hôpitaly ve? → And have I arrived at the hospital?
6. Aan, mbola tsy tonga. → No, not yet.
Mankany — to go to  |  Mbola — still  |  Dia — then  |  Misy — there is  |  Tonga — arrived/come  |  Mbola tsy — not yet  |  Aan — No / Een — Yes  |  Ianao — you

B

1. Amin'ny faran'ny fidinana. → At the end of the descent.
2. Mivily ankavia ianao. → Turn left.
3. Misy sampanan-dàlana. → There is a crossroads.
4. Mivily mianavaratra. → Turn north.
5. Mandeha kely dia misy tetezana. → Walk a little and there is a bridge.
6. Mbola lavitra avy eo ve? → Is it still far from there?
7. Tsy lavitra avy eo. → Not far from there.
Farany — end  |  Lavitra — far  |  Avy eo — from there  |  Mianavaratra — go north  |  Mandeha kely — walk a little  |  Tsy lavitra — not far  |  Avaratra — North

D

1. Miampita ny tetezana ianao dia manohy. → Cross the bridge and continue.
2. Misy fiviliana ankavia. → There is a turning on the left.
3. Tsy mivily. → Do not turn.
4. Manohy mahitsy. → Continue straight on.
5. Manaraka ny ankavanana ve? → Then follow on the right?
6. Een, miakatra kely. → Yes, go up a little.
7. Misy fiviliana amin'ny ankavia. → There is a turning on the left.
8. Trano misy etazy amin'ny ankavanana. → A multi-storey building on the right.
Fiviliana — turning / a turn  |  Manaraka — to follow  |  Mivily — turn  |  Trano — house  |  Trano misy etazy — apartment / multi-storey building  |  Fa — but  |  Tsy mivily — do not turn  |  Etazy — floor / storey

E

1. Ny hôpitaly ve? → The hospital?
2. Efa tsy lavitra ny hôpitaly. → The hospital is no longer far.
3. Avy eo amin'ny trano misy etazy. → From the multi-storey building.
4. Manohy mahitsy. → Continue straight on.
5. Mandalo bazary sy sekoly ianao. → You pass by the market and the school.
6. Manohy kely ianao. → Continue a little further.
7. Mahita fiviliana amin'ny ankavanana. → You will see a turning on the right.
8. Misy vavahady lehibe. → There is a large gate.
9. Ny vavahadin'ny hôpitaly. → The gate of the hospital.
Efa tsy — no longer  |  Kely — a little  |  Vavahady — gate  |  Avy eo amin'ny — from the …  |  Mahita — see / find  |  Lehibe — big

F

1. Misaotra betsaka tompoko. → Thank you very much.
2. Tsisy fisaorana. → You're welcome.
3. Raha very ianao. → If you get lost.
4. Afaka manontany olona ianao. → You can ask someone.
5. "Aiza ny hôpitaly azafady?" → "Where is the hospital please?"
Raha — if  |  Afaka — can / able to  |  Manontany olona — ask someone  |  Misaotra betsaka — thank you very much  |  Very — to be lost  |  Manontany — to ask  |  Tsisy fisaorana — you're welcome
