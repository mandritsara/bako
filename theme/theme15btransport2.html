2.2 Verbs

Malagasy English Examples
MandehaTo go / walk / travelHandeha any Frantsa izahay.(We will go to France.)
Miala / MiaingaTo leave / departMiainga amin'ny 7 ny fiaramanidina.(The plane leaves at 7.)
MialaTo get off (alight)Aiza no miala ianao?(Where are you getting off?)
TongaTo arriveEfa tonga ny taxi-brousse.(The taxi-brousse has already arrived.)
Mijanona (off) / Mijanogno (tsm)To stopMijanona eto ve ny bus 163?(Does bus 163 stop here?)
MifindraTo change (means of transport / place)Mila mifindra bus ve?(Is it necessary to change bus?)Afaka mifindra toerana ianao.(You can change seats.)
MidinaTo get off / go downMidina ny mpandeha.(The passengers get off.)
Mivoaka (off) / Miboaka (tsm)To go outAfaka mivoaka ve azafady?(Can we go out, please?)
Miditra (off) / Miditry (tsm)To enterAfaka miditra ny mpandeha.(The passengers can enter.)
MiakatraTo get on / intoMiakatra ao anaty bus izy.(He/She gets on the bus.)
MilahatraTo queueMilahatra bus ny mpianatra.(The pupils queue for the bus.)
Miandry (off) / Mandigny (tsm)To waitMiandry taxi aho.(I am waiting for a taxi.)
