Translate the following sentences into English

  1. Mampianatra malagasy zaho
  2. Manoratra amin'ny solaitrabe izy
  3. Mamaky boky i Soa
  4. Mihaino ny mpampianatra ny mpianatra
  5. Nianjera izy
  6. Milahatra ny mpianatra
  7. Misoma ny mpianatra
  8. Afaka manontany ianao
  9. Mieritreritra izy
  10. Manazava lesona ny mpampianatra
  11. Miditra ny mpampianatra dia mipetraka izy
Answers
1. Mampianatra malagasy zaho → I teach Malagasy
2. Manoratra amin'ny solaitrabe izy → He is writing on the blackboard
3. Mamaky boky i Soa → Soa is reading a book
4. Mihaino ny mpampianatra ny mpianatra → The students are listening to the teacher
5. Nianjera izy → He fell down
6. Milahatra ny mpianatra → The students line up
7. Misoma ny mpianatra → The students play
8. Afaka manontany ianao → You can ask a question
9. Mieritreritra izy → He is thinking
10. Manazava lesona ny mpampianatra → The teacher explains the lesson
11. Miditra ny mpampianatra dia mipetraka izy → The teacher enters and sits down
