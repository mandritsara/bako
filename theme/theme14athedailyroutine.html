Theme 14A — Ny andavanandro

The Daily Routine

Background

Every day we do almost the same things: we get up, we get ready, we eat, we go to work or to school, and so on.

But not everyone has the same daily routine. It depends on a person's activity, or even on where they live.

Many Malagasy people are farmers. Their routine: they get up before sunrise and start work while it is still cool, and they go to bed early. Their routine is more physical and seasonal, depending on the climate, and farmers do not do just one and the same job (looking after the animals, working in the fields, repairing tools, selling produce, listening to the radio for the news and for serials with the family).

We will try to discover the essential basic verbs, then other frequent and useful verbs, so that you can tell what you do during the day, saying 'I get up at . . .', etc.

3. Describe your daily activities

Choose verbs from the tables above and describe your daily activities by making sentences (refer to Theme 10 on times if necessary).

verb – aho / zaho – amin'ny . . . .

(I – verb – at . . . . . . . .)

Ex: Mifoha aho amin'ny 5 sy sasany (off)

Mionjogno zaho amin'ny 5 sy sasany (tsm)

(I wake up at 5.30)

NB. You can use conjunctions such as "SY" = and; "AVY EO" = then.
