Theme 14B — Ny andavanandro

The Daily Routine (continued)

1. Text: Rakoto the farmer

Listen to the daily routine of a Malagasy farmer called Rakoto. Try to say it in your own language, using the vocabulary after the text.

Mpamboly Rakoto. Mipetraka any ambanivohitra izy.

Mifoha amin'ny 4 ora maraina izy dia miomana sy misakafo maraina.

Amin'ny 5 ora, manome sakafo ny biby fiompy, ny akoho sy ny kisoa izy.

Mandeha miasa any an-tsaha izy amin'ny 6 ora eo ho eo.

Misakafo atoandro izy amin'ny 12 ora atoandro avy eo miala sasatra.

Miverina miasa izy rehefa mangatsiatsiaka, amin'ny 3 ora tolakandro sy mody amin'ny 5 ora hariva.

Manome sakafo ny biby fiompy indray avy eo mandro izy.

Misakafo hariva izy sy ny fianakaviany amin'ny 7 ora hariva.

Mihaino radio avy eo matory amin'ny 8 ora alina satria hifoha amin'ny 4 maraina rahampitso.

NB: In Malagasy, times are expressed from 1 to 12. To tell them apart, the part of the day is added after the number, such as maraina (morning) or hariva (afternoon / evening).

Adding the word ora, which means hour, after the number is optional and does not change how the time is understood.

3. Exercise: translate the following sentences into Malagasy

# English Malagasy
1.Rakoto lives in the village.
2.He is a farmer.
3.He wakes up, then gets ready and has breakfast.
4.Rakoto feeds the animals at 5 in the morning.
5.He goes to the fields.
6.He has lunch at midday.
7.Rakoto takes a break after lunch.
8.It is cool at 3 in the afternoon.
9.He goes home at 5 in the evening.
10.Rakoto has a shower.
11.Rakoto has dinner with his family at around 7 in the evening.
12.He listens to the radio, then goes to bed at 8.
13.A farmer wakes up at 4 in the morning.

Answers
