Theme 11B — Asking for Directions (continued 2)

Manontany sy manoro làlana sy toerana — Tohiny 2

Asking for and giving directions to a road or place (continued 2)

Introduction

In Madagascar, only in large towns do streets usually have names or numbers. In most places, people use landmarks instead.

1. Questions for asking about places and directions

When asking, Malagasy speakers always use azafady (please) first, then either ask the question directly or begin with an introductory phrase like "Could you show/tell me…"

Azafady, aiza ny hôpitaly eto? (off)

Azafady, aiza moa lôpitaly aketo? (tsm)

→ "Please, where is the hospital here?"

Azafady, afaka manoro ahy ny hôpitaly eto ve ianao? (off)

Azafady, afaka manoro zaho ny lôpitaly aketo moa anao? (tsm)

→ "Please, could you show me where the hospital is here?"

To practise, replace (place) with Mandritsara (for #1 to #7) and with ny banky (the bank) for #6, #8 and #9.

# Malagasy (off / tsm) English meaning
1 An'inona eto azafady? (off) / An'ino moa aketo azafady? (tsm) "What is this place, please?" / "Where is this place, please?"
2 Azafady, aiza ny làlana mandeha any (place)? "Please, where is the road that goes to …?"
3 Azafady, ity ve ny làlana mankany (place)? (off) / Azafady, tôho moa làlana mankany (place)? (tsm) "Please, is this the right way to …?"
4 Azafady, ahoana ny fomba andehanana any (place)? (off) / Azafady, manakory mandeha any (place)? (tsm) "Please, how do I get to …?"
5 (Place) ve eto azafady? (off) / (Place) moa aketo azafady? (tsm) "Is … here, please?"
6 Mbola lavitra avy eto ve (place) azafady? (off) / Mbola lavitra avaketo moa (place) azafady? (tsm) "Please, is … still far from here?"
7 Adiny firy avy eto ny (place) azafady? (off) / Firy lera avaketo moa ny (place) azafady? (tsm) "Please, how many hours from here is …?"
8 Aiza ny (place) eto azafady? (off) / Aiza ny (place) aketo azafady? (tsm) "Please, where is … here?"
9 Azafady, misy (place) ve akaiky eto? (off) / Misy (place) moa marigny aketo azafady? (tsm) "Please, is there a … near here?"

3. Practice — Understanding and building sentences

Try to understand the structure of these examples, then write your own sentences describing the place where you live, using vocabulary from section 2.

Examples — "It's the…"

Ny fiampitana akaikin'ny fiangonana. → "It's the crossing near the church."

Ny fiakarana aorinan'ny hôpitaly. → "It's the ascent after the hospital."

Ny tetezana anelanelan'ny fiangonana sy ny hôpitaly. → "It's the bridge between the church and the hospital."

Ny fijanonan'ny bus ankavian'ny fiangonana. → "It's the bus stop to the left of the church."

Ny fiangonana amin'ny faran'ny làlana. → "It's the church at the end of the road."

Ny hôpitaly ampitan'ny fiangonana. → "It's the hospital opposite the church."

Examples — "The … is …"

Anoloan'ny hôpitaly ny fiampitana. → "The crossing is in front of the hospital."

Amoron-dàlana ny fiangonana. → "The church is by the roadside."

Andamosin'ny hôpitaly ny fijanonan'ny bus. → "The bus stop is behind the hospital."

Amin'ny faran'ny làlana ankavia ny fiangonana. → "The church is at the end of the street on the left."

Akaikin'ny tetezana ny hôpitaly. → "The hospital is next to the bridge."

Amin'ny zoron-dàlana ny hôpitaly. → "The hospital is at the corner of the street."
